Phogram's message reactions are Google's Noto Animated Emoji
(https://googlefonts.github.io/noto-emoji-animation/), Copyright Google LLC,
licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Each file is the animation's Lottie JSON as Google publishes it, compressed with gzip
(the .tgs form Telegram plays); the app scales a copy to half size for the reaction's
center icon. The files were taken once on 2026-11-19 and ship with the app, so the app
never fetches them.
